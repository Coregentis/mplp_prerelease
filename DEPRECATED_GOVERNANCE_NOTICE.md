# Governance Deprecation Notice

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Status:** FINAL

---

## Declaration

All internal governance documents, policies, checklists, and procedural documents
created before 2026-01-01 are hereby declared **VOID** and **non-binding**.

This notice applies to all internal governance drafts, policies, checklists,
and procedural documents, **regardless of file location or naming**.

---

## Scope of Deprecation

The following are explicitly deprecated and carry no authority:

- All files previously in `project-governance/`
- All alignment documents (ALIGNMENT_v1.x, DRV reports, etc.)
- All governance specifications (SURFACE_MODEL, DRV_POLICY, etc.)
- All truth mapping ledgers and baselines
- Any document referencing the above as authoritative

---

## Replacement

This deprecation is superseded by the **Three Constitution Documents**:

1. `ENTRY_MODEL_SPEC.md` — Defines the three-entry model
2. `DOCUMENT_FORMAT_SPEC.md` — Defines document structure and rules
3. `FROZEN_HEADER_SPEC.md` — Defines frozen header usage

These documents are located in `governance/` and are the **sole source of
governance authority** for MPLP documentation.

---

## Non-Publication Statement

None of the deprecated governance documents were ever published externally.
This deprecation has no impact on any external commitments or published specifications.
